Good Questions

Questions Buyers & Sellers Ask Me

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan program. Some buyers qualify for low-down-payment options; others put more down based on their goals. A lender can help find the best fit.

What credit score do I need?

Requirements vary by lender and loan type. Some programs are more flexible than others, so it's worth speaking with a lender before assuming you do or don't qualify.

Should I get pre-approved before looking?

Usually, yes. A pre-approval establishes your budget and puts you in a stronger position when it's time to make an offer.

How long does buying take?

Once under contract, closing is often around 30–45 days, depending on financing and the transaction. The search itself can be shorter or longer.

How much is my home worth?

Online estimates are a starting point, but accurate pricing also considers condition, updates, location, recent comparable sales, and current competition.

What should I do before listing?

It depends on the property. I'll help identify what's worth repairing, updating, cleaning, or leaving alone before you spend money unnecessarily.

Do I need to sell before I buy?

Not always. It depends on your finances, current mortgage, equity, and loan approval. We can review your situation with a lender and create the right sequence.

Can I make an offer before my current home sells?

Potentially. Options may include purchasing first, using a home-sale contingency, or coordinating the transactions closely.

How does my Realtor get paid?

Compensation and brokerage fees vary by transaction and are addressed in the applicable agreements. I'll explain the structure clearly before you commit to anything.
